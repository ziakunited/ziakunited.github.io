Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

1. Who we are

Z7 Brain is an internal analytics tool operated by Ziak United (contact: varamisra1@gmail.com).

2. Data we collect

Only with your permission through TikTok Login Kit, and only for the account that signs in:

  • Basic profile: open ID, display name, avatar, profile link
  • Account statistics: follower, following, likes and video counts
  • Public videos: video ID, title/description, duration, publish time and engagement counts (views, likes, comments, shares)

We do not collect data about your followers, viewers or any other TikTok user, and we do not collect passwords.

3. How we use it

To create private dashboards and daily performance reports for the account owner. We do not sell, rent or share the data with third parties and do not use it for advertising.

4. Storage and security

Data and access tokens are stored on the channel owner's own computer and are not uploaded to any public server. Access tokens are kept in a private folder that is excluded from sharing.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the account remains connected. You can revoke access in TikTok's app permissions at any time, and email us to have all stored data deleted; we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
