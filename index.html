Z7 Brain

Z7 Brain is a private analytics tool used by the team behind Ziak United, a Thai football storytelling channel on TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

It lets the channel owner sign in with their own TikTok account and read their own profile statistics and video performance (views, likes, comments, shares) to build internal daily reports. It is not offered to the public and does not post, comment or message on anyone's behalf.
